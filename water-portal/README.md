# Jal Sanrakshan: Water Conservation and Sustainable Water Management

CEP / FP project, S>B. Jain Institute of Technology Management and Research, Nagpur. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript (no backend).

Pages: Home, Learn, Tracker (with calculator), Schemes, Quiz, Resources. Includes dark mode, water fact of the day, and links that open Google, YouTube, Wikipedia and official websites in a new tab.

Team: Akanshri Kailash Borkar, Anshul Prakash Kumbhare, Anushka Vikas Thakre, Arpita Ramesh Ikhankar. Guide: Ms. Pratiksha Bhimte.
